In this project for airline analysis, 4 business processes have been identified:

1] Flight Operations 
2] Flight Booking
3] Flight Checkin
4] Baggage

Steps:
A]  For all 4 business processes, individual Star Schema was developed
B] After successful creation of star schema, all business processes were combined and we get the final Snowflake Schema
C] After making sure for intigrity of table names, attribute names and correct data; dashboards for each process were created.

With this project, we dive deep in concepts of Star and Snowflake Schema for Data Modelling and learn how data is unified for different Business Processes.
